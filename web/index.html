Sleeper Lineup Compare

Scores players with your Sleeper league's own rules.
  1. Find your league. Type your Sleeper username to pick from your leagues, or paste a league ID. Scoring rules come from that league.
  2. Build two lineups. Search a name under Team A and Team B. Include kickers and defenses. Rename the teams if you like.
  3. Pick a time frame and tap Score. Choose a week, or "Season total" to add up every week. The higher total is highlighted.
  4. Share it. "Share link" copies a URL with both lineups so a friend sees the same matchup.

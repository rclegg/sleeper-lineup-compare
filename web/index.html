Sleeper Lineup Compare

Scores players with your Sleeper league's own rules.
How it works
  1. Enter your Sleeper username. Pick your league if you have more than one. Team A fills with your starters and Team B with this week's opponent.
  2. Change anything. Pick another manager in a team's "Fill from" list, search to add or remove players, or choose a different week.
  3. Scores update on their own using your league's scoring rules. "Share link" sends the matchup to a friend.
